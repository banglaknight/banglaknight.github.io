The task runs on CodaBench in two phases. {{CB_STATUS}} All data is on the Data page.

{{PHASES}}

{{i:sword}}Development phase · Oct 17, 2026 – Jan 7, 2027

  1. 1

    {{i:user-plus}}Register on CodaBench

    Create a CodaBench account and click Register on the {{CB:competition page}}. Approval is automatic. We send announcements to your CodaBench email; it is never shown publicly.

    Open now · Closes Jan 17, 2027

  2. 2

    {{i:download}}Download the starter kit

    Train and dev sets, the blind dev-test set, a starter notebook with a simple baseline, and the prepare_submission_file notebook, all on the Data page.

    Available now

  3. 3

    {{i:cpu}}Build your system

    Start from the starter notebook and train on the train set.

  4. 4

    {{i:play}}Practice on CodaBench

    Predict the blind dev-test set, turn the predictions into a submission file with the prepare_submission_file notebook, and submit it on CodaBench. Each submission asks for your team name, affiliation and study level, which appear on the leaderboard. You see your dev-test score and know your file format works.

    Until Jan 7, 2027

{{i:shield}}Test phase · Jan 8 – Jan 24, 2027

  1. 5

    {{i:inbox}}Get the test set

    New prompts you have not seen, without labels, posted on the Data page. Run your final system on them.

    Released Jan 8, 2027

  2. 6

    {{i:upload}}Submit your final predictions

    Make the submission file with the prepare_submission_file notebook and upload it on CodaBench. Your latest finished submission is the one that is ranked; test scores stay hidden until the results.

    Deadline Jan 24, 2027, 11:59 PM AoE

{{i:trophy}}After the competition

  1. 7

    {{i:trophy}}See the results and write your paper

    The official ranking comes out on Feb 3, 2027. Then describe your system in a paper. Paper Submission

    Paper due Feb 15, 2027
