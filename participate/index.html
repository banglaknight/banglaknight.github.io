Participate

From registration on CodaBench to your final submission.

The task runs on CodaBench. Registration opens on October 20, 2026; the competition link will be posted here that day.

  1. 1

    Register on CodaBench

    Create a CodaBench account and join the BanglaKnight competition.

    Opens Oct 20, 2026 · Closes Jan 24, 2027

  2. 2

    Download the starter kit

    Train and dev sets, a starter notebook with a simple baseline, and the prepare_submission_file notebook. What's in it

    Oct 20, 2026

  3. 3

    Build your system

    Start from the starter notebook, train on the train set, and check your scores on the dev set.

    Oct 20, 2026 – Jan 14, 2027

  4. 4

    Predict the test set

    Download the test set (no labels) and run your system on it.

    Test data released Jan 15, 2027

  5. 5

    Make your submission file

    Run the prepare_submission_file notebook on your predictions. It writes the file in the format CodaBench expects.

  6. 6

    Submit on CodaBench

    Upload the file on the CodaBench competition page.

    Deadline Jan 31, 2027, 11:59 PM AoE

  7. 7

    Write your paper

    Results come out on Feb 3, 2027. Then describe your system in a paper. Paper Submission

    Paper due Feb 15, 2027

Questions? See Organizers for contact details.
