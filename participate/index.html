The task runs on CodaBench in two phases. Registration opens on October 20, 2026; the competition link will be posted here that day.

Phase 1

Development phase

Oct 20, 2026 – Jan 15, 2027

Data
Training and dev sets, plus the starter kit
You
Build and tune your system
CodaBench
Submit predictions on a blind dev-test set to see your score and check your file format. Practice only: it does not count toward the final ranking.

Test phase

Jan 15 – Jan 31, 2027

Data

You

CodaBench

Development phase · Oct 20, 2026 – Jan 15, 2027

  1. 1

    Register on CodaBench

    Create a CodaBench account and join the BanglaKnight competition.

    Opens Oct 20, 2026 · Closes Jan 24, 2027

  2. 2

    Download the starter kit

    Train and dev sets, a starter notebook with a simple baseline, and the prepare_submission_file notebook. What's in it

    Oct 20, 2026

  3. 3

    Build your system

    Start from the starter notebook and train on the train set.

  4. 4

    Practice on CodaBench

    Predict the blind dev-test set on CodaBench, turn the predictions into a submission file with the prepare_submission_file notebook, and submit it. You see your dev-test score and know your file format works.

    Until Jan 15, 2027

Test phase · Jan 15 – Jan 31, 2027

  1. 5

    Get the test set

    New prompts you have not seen, without labels. Run your final system on them.

    Released Jan 15, 2027

  2. 6

    Submit your final predictions

    Make the submission file with the prepare_submission_file notebook and upload it on CodaBench. This is the submission that is ranked.

    Deadline Jan 31, 2027, 11:59 PM AoE

After the competition

  1. 7

    See the results and write your paper

    The official ranking comes out on Feb 3, 2027. Then describe your system in a paper. Paper Submission

    Paper due Feb 15, 2027
